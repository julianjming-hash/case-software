# COLLECTION NOTICE

Administrative audit retention is indefinite; participant and response data has its separate 24-hour purge. Operational copies and provider recovery history are separate from current-store deletion. Account deletion is Auth-first, with a thirty-day restore window; unresolved deletion claims require manual reconciliation. Cloudflare Durable Object PITR has a reported 30 days recovery window; deleteAll() erases the current store, not proven recovery-history erasure. post-deletion recovery-history erasure is unverified. The recovery-history compatibility remains unverified. PITR is unavailable in local development. DO recovery-history erasure after deleteAll and the seven-day contract: Not verified. This recovery-history boundary is unresolved before any pilot. These are software boundary statements, not an institutional procurement approval or a verified provider erasure receipt.

> Issue reports and optional screenshots are visible to deployment administrators and their reporter. Security or privacy incidents are restricted to the designated incident administrator. The browser picker captures a single unfiltered screenshot into a local draft; nothing uploads before Send. Preview or remove it before sending.

> Unresolved issues are retained; evidence is deleted thirty days after resolution. Participant original prose, screenshots and identity links follow the room end plus twenty-four-hour deadline. A reviewed nonpersonal engineering summary may remain until thirty days after resolution. Anonymous report access ends with room capability. Contact requires your separate consent. Reports are not automatically sent to AI services.

> Case is run by its owner for facilitator-led clinical teaching. The facilitator runs your session and controls what the room sees.

> When you join, Case asks for a display name and an avatar. It records your answers during the session. It also handles connection data needed to connect your phone and keep the room in step.

> The facilitator sees your name, avatar and answers. Names and avatars appear in the projector lobby. During play, avatars appear; names require Show names. Closed responses, including free text, appear without names on the projector. The facilitator can hide them.

> Case runs on Cloudflare's edge network and stores session records in Sydney. Cloudflare may process information outside Australia.

> After repeated incorrect room codes, Cloudflare Turnstile may check this join page. Its script has access to this document and may read the entered room code, display name, avatar and browser storage. Cloudflare also processes network information such as your IP address. The check does not run in the session play or recap page.

> Names and per-person answers in the live room and session records are deleted within 25 hours after the session ends. Encrypted backups and connection logs expire within 7 days.

> Anonymous usage and connection metrics are retained for three months. These analytics points contain no names, answers, room or participant IDs, tokens, IP addresses or URLs.

> Do not enter patient details or information that identifies another person in your name or answers.

> You can use a name the room can call you without using your legal name, choose an avatar, and change either during the session. You can choose whether to answer a decision. Ask the facilitator if you have concerns about showing names on the projector.

> For a privacy question, or to ask about access or correction, contact the facilitator or your hospital teaching organiser. They can put you in touch with the Case operator.
